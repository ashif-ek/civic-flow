# 01 — Project Overview

## CivicFlow — AI-Powered Public Grievance Management Platform

- **Frontend Application (Vercel)**: [https://civicflow-v1.vercel.app/](https://civicflow-v1.vercel.app/)
- **Backend API & Swagger Documentation (Railway)**: [https://civicflow-backend-production.up.railway.app/api/docs/](https://civicflow-backend-production.up.railway.app/api/docs/)

---

CivicFlow is a centralized, end-to-end public grievance redressal portal. It allows citizens to lodge civic complaints regarding public infrastructure, health, water supply, electricity, transport, and municipal services across Kerala's 14 districts.

The application features:
- **Direct Citizen Grievance Submission** with unique alphanumeric tracking numbers (`KL-GRV-XXXXXX`).
- **AI-Powered Triage & Classification** utilizing Google Gemini API to recommend departments, urgency priorities, and summary briefings.
- **Apex CMO Administrator Console** for state-wide grievance tracking, filtering, department routing, and grievance escalation.
- **Dedicated Department Officer Desks** for field investigation logging, status updates, and formal Action-Taken Report submissions.
- **Public Audit Trail & Citizen Feedback** allowing any citizen to monitor complaint redressal and score satisfaction (1–5 stars) upon resolution.

> **Independent Demonstration Disclaimer**: CivicFlow is a recruitment prototype created for technical evaluation. It is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Kerala.
